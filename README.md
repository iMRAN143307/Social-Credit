## Slack

Join #the-revolution to get started

## Commands

- /social-credit to check your social credit
- Use sentences such as "I support capitalism" to lower your social credit
- Use sentences such as "I support communism" to raise your social credit

## Reader

- Set wifi SSID and password in config.h
- You can also configure pins here if you used different pins, or use the pins from config.h to wire up your project.
